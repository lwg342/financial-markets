01 / Executive overview

Market segmentation and trading infrastructure

Baseline snapshot · 2025

China's domestic capital markets combine exchange-based securities trading, an extensive interbank debt market and centrally organised listed derivatives venues. Trading permissions and execution procedures differ substantially across segments.

Market access and trading constraints are best analysed jointly with instrument design, participant eligibility, venue microstructure and settlement arrangements—rather than treated as one uniform set of market-wide rules.

02 / Venue architecture

Exchange-traded vs. OTC market organisation

CENTRALISED VENUES

Exchange-traded markets

Buy orders
→
Exchange
protocols
←
Sell orders

Trading under exchange rules with defined order types, execution methods, risk controls and clearing arrangements. Relevant for listed equities and standardised futures/options. Exchange trades may include auctions, continuous matching and other permitted protocols.

DECENTRALISED / ELECTRONIC OTC

Interbank debt market

Dealer / investor
↔
RFQ / quoting
electronic systems
↔
Counterparty

Quote-driven, request-for-quote and other electronic trading protocols. The interbank bond market is regulated and electronically operated: OTC does not imply informal, unregulated or exclusively bilateral offline trading.

04 / Market profiles

Institutional structure by asset class

Roles vary by strategy, venue and regulatory status

Equity securities

01
Order-driven · Exchange
Economic claim
Residual ownership in listed companies; primary issuance is distinct from secondary share transfers.
Buy-side and sell-side composition
Both buying and selling orders originate from investors and intermediated accounts. “Buy-side/sell-side” institution labels should not be confused with the buyer and seller in each trade.
Market topology
SSE, SZSE and BSE; board-specific regimes and exchange trading protocols.
Baseline constraints
Eligible account access, session rules, resale timing and security-specific price limits.
Analytical distinction · Issuer financing ≠ secondary-market transfer.

Debt securities

02
Interbank OTC + Exchange
Economic claim
Contractual debt obligations, with coupons, principal payments and credit/rate exposure.
Participant ecology
Commercial banks, insurers, funds, securities firms, dealers and approved investors.
Market topology
Interbank market, exchange bond markets and bank-counter channels, with distinct admission mechanisms.
Baseline constraints
Investor qualification, eligible bond universes, platform membership and trading/settlement arrangements.
Analytical distinction · OTC vs exchange is about market organisation, not security type.

Financial derivatives

03
Listed · Margin-based
Instrument design
Standardised financial futures and listed options; examples include stock-index and government-bond contracts.
Participant ecology
Risk managers, institutional hedgers, arbitrageurs, market makers and directional traders.
Market topology
China Financial Futures Exchange (CFFEX).
Baseline constraints
Product-specific eligibility, margin, daily settlement, position limits and contract expiry.
Analytical distinction · A short futures position does not require pre-ownership of the underlying asset.

Commodity derivatives

04
Listed · Physical / cash settlement
Instrument design
Exchange-listed futures and options linked to metals, energy and agricultural commodities.
Participant ecology
Industrial firms, commodity merchants, risk hedgers, arbitrageurs and financial investors.
Market topology
SHFE, INE, DCE, CZCE and GFEX.
Baseline constraints
Contract-specific margin, price bands, concentration/position limits, delivery rules and expiry.
Analytical distinction · Commercial hedging and speculative positions may be opposite sides of the same contract.

05 / Synthesis

Implications for the constraint research programme

Venue segmentation

Trading rules attach to a market and execution system. A single asset family, particularly bonds, may span several distinct venues.

Eligibility differentiation

Investor category, product access, account configuration and membership determine the feasible trading universe.

Instrument-specific rules

Execution, clearing, price limits, resale timing and delivery rules depend on the security or contract design.

Research boundary: This is a first-stage institutional overview. No comprehensive rule enumeration, portfolio optimisation, causal estimation or automated monitoring is claimed.
