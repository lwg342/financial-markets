# China Financial Markets — Market Structure Review

**Research note | Coverage: Mainland China | Baseline: 2025 data | Updated: 25 October 2026**

An introductory market-structure reference focused on **traded instruments, participant composition, venue architecture, execution protocols, market access and baseline trading constraints**. Prepared for academic discussion; not an exhaustive regulatory inventory or investment advice.

## Coverage

| Segment | Venue architecture | Participant composition | Reference metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listed equities (A-shares) | SSE / SZSE / BSE; exchange order book and other permitted protocols | Retail and institutional investors | Shanghai + Shenzhen A-share market capitalisation: **RMB 107.84 tn** (2025 year-end; excluding BSE) |
| Debt securities | Interbank market (electronic OTC); exchange-listed bonds; bank-counter distribution | Banks, asset managers, insurers, securities firms; eligible other investors | China bond market outstanding custody balance: **RMB 196.7 tn** (2025 year-end) |
| Financial futures & options | CFFEX; centrally organised listed derivatives | Hedgers, directional traders, arbitrageurs, market makers | CFFEX 2025 turnover: **RMB 255.19 tn** |
| Commodity futures & options | SHFE / INE / DCE / CZCE / GFEX; listed derivatives | Industrial hedgers, traders, investors | Commodity-exchange 2025 turnover: **RMB 511.06 tn** |

**Statistical warning:** Market capitalisation, custody balance and annual turnover represent different quantities (stocks vs flow). Listed-derivatives annual turnover includes **both futures and options**, counted on a single-sided basis. The data are not a ranking of comparative market depth.
