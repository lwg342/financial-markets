## C. Three main findings to communicate

**1. Market structure is not homogeneous.** Listed A-shares are principally exchange-traded; Chinese debt securities circulate through interbank OTC, exchange and bank-counter arrangements; standardised futures/options trade on designated exchanges.

**2. Buyers and sellers are roles in a trade, not necessarily separate permanent groups.** Individual and institutional investors can buy or sell listed shares or bonds. For futures, hedgers, speculators and arbitrageurs may take either the long or the short side. “Buy side” and “sell side” in financial-industry terminology have a distinct institutional meaning.

**3. Access is conditional and layered.** Constraints can be grouped into market membership/account eligibility, product-specific qualifications, execution/timing parameters, and post-trade requirements such as margin, clearing, settlement or delivery.

## D. Market data — explain the measurement choice

- A-shares: **RMB 107.84 trillion** Shanghai and Shenzhen A-share market capitalisation (2025 year-end, BSE excluded); secondary reporting of ChinaClear annual statistics.
- Bonds: **RMB 196.7 trillion** outstanding bond custody balance (2025 year-end); PBoC.
- CFFEX: **RMB 255.19 trillion** 2025 transaction turnover (futures + options, single-sided); China Futures Association.
- Commodity exchanges: **RMB 511.06 trillion** 2025 transaction turnover (futures + options, single-sided); sum of SHFE, INE, DCE, CZCE and GFEX.

**Qualification:** They are not four observations of the same economic variable. Do not plot them on a common comparable market-size axis or calculate market shares from them.

## E. Illustrative investor-centric examples

**Equity:** A domestic retail investor executing a secondary-market A-share purchase must access an eligible securities account. Exchange-specific timing and board rules affect the execution opportunity set; the investor is not purchasing new issuance from the listed company in every secondary trade.

**Bond:** An institution considering a government bond must choose the feasible venue / trading account architecture. Bank-interbank electronic OTC protocols and exchange-based bond execution are not identical. Bond valuations and yields are a separate, later-stage analytical topic.

**Futures:** A user can express long or short exposure using a margined derivative position, but product eligibility, margin and risk controls apply. Commodity delivery constraints are not necessarily relevant to every financial futures product.
